Velocity App: first look

Four screens covering one set from start to finish, plus the strength profile and settings. Tap the exercise chips, the load field, the bar or system power switch, and the kg or lb switch in Settings to see how they respond. Nothing here is measured; the numbers are examples.

What's real so far

The measurement behind screens 2 and 3 exists as the prototype in the repository. The screens themselves are a design to react to.

Other equipment

When no plate is in view, the setup screen would switch to body tracking for dumbbells, kettlebells and bodyweight work, starting with rep counting.

Program tab

A placeholder for the later programming integration: planned sets, and loads suggested from your velocity.
