Velocity App: first look

Screens 1–6 cover one set from start to finish, plus the strength profile and settings. Screens 7–11 show the program built from your profile. Tap the exercise chips, the load field, the bar or system power switch, and the kg or lb switch in Settings to see how they respond. Nothing here is measured; the numbers are examples.

Program

Programming from your profile

Your plan is written in %1RM. The app turns each percentage into a load and a target velocity from your strength profile, then adjusts for today's readiness. Tap the readiness readings on screen 8, and the goal, length and training-level options on screen 11.

What's real so far

The measurement behind screens 2 and 3 exists as the prototype in the repository. The screens themselves are a design to react to.

Other equipment

When no plate is in view, the setup screen would switch to body tracking for dumbbells, kettlebells and bodyweight work, starting with rep counting.

Program numbers

Screens 7–11 use the draft values from the programming spec and an example profile: estimated 1RM 170 kg, mean velocity = 1.62 − 0.008 × load. They change when you edit the spec.
